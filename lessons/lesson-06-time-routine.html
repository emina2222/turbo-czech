6. Time & Daily Routine

Tell the time, name the days of the week, and talk about your work shifts — essential for scheduling, punctuality, and understanding your roster.

💡 Tip

The days of the week are not capitalized in Czech, unlike English: pondělí, úterý, středa…

Step 2 · Grammar focus

Telling the time and using "v" for "at"

Time is said with Je (It is) + the hour + "hodina/hodiny/hodin" (following the same 1 / 2-4 / 5+ noun-ending pattern from Lesson 2):

CzechEnglish
Je jedna hodina.It's one o'clock.
Jsou tři hodiny.It's three o'clock.
Je pět hodin.It's five o'clock.

To say "at [time]", use v: Začínám v sedm hodin. (I start at seven o'clock.)

📌 Good to know

Shift talk at work: "Mám ranní směnu." (I have the morning shift.) "Končím v šest večer." (I finish at six in the evening.)
