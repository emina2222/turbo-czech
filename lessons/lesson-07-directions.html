7. Directions & Places

Ask where something is, understand basic directions, and name common places around a workplace — so you can find the restroom, the cafeteria, or the exit on day one.

🎯 By the end of this lesson you can:

  • Ask where something is
  • Understand basic directions
  • Name common places around a workplace
Step 1 · Vocabulary

New words

Tap the speaker to hear each word. Try repeating it out loud.

  • kde/gdeh/where
  • tady/TA-di/here
  • tam/tam/there
  • vlevo/VLE-vo/(on the) left
  • vpravo/VPRA-vo/(on the) right
  • rovně/ROV-nye/straight ahead
  • vchod/vkhod/entrance
  • východ/VEE-khod/exit
  • toaleta/TO-a-le-ta/restroom
  • kancelář/KAN-tse-lahrzh/office
  • jídelna, kantýna/YEE-del-na, kan-TEE-na/cafeteria, canteen
  • šatna/SHAT-na/locker room
  • parkoviště/PAR-ko-vish-tye/parking lot

💡 Tip

Learn vlevo, vpravo, and rovně by heart — they're the three words you'll hear most often when asking for directions at work.

Step 2 · Grammar focus

Asking "kde" and answering with v / na

"Kde je…?" (Where is…?) is your key question. Answers usually use v (in) or na (at/on) before the place:

CzechEnglish
Kancelář je vlevo.The office is on the left.
Toaleta je tam, vpravo.The restroom is over there, on the right.
Auto je na parkovišti.The car is in the parking lot.
Jsem v šatně.I'm in the locker room.

📌 Good to know

You don't need to master every grammar rule behind "v" and "na" yet — for now just learn each phrase as a chunk (v kanceláři, na parkovišti, v šatně) and you'll be understood.
