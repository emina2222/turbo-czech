Food & Drink

Name common foods and drinks, say what you like and want, and order simply at a canteen or shop — perfect for your lunch break.

💡 Tip

Dám si… is the easiest, most natural way to order anything — food, drinks, at a canteen counter or a restaurant. Just add the item you want after it.

Step 2 · Grammar focus

The verb chtít (to want)

Chtít lets you say what you want — very handy when ordering food or asking for something at work.

PronounChtít (to want)
já (I)chci
ty (you, informal)chceš
on/ona/ono (he/she/it)chce
my (we)chceme
vy (you, formal/plural)chcete
oni/ony (they)chtějí

📌 Good to know

Mít rád (to like, literally "to have glad") changes with the SPEAKER's gender, not the object. A male speaker says "Mám rád kávu" (I like coffee); a female speaker says "Mám ráda čaj" (I like tea) — notice rád/ráda agrees with who is speaking.
