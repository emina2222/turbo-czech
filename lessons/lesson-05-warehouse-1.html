Warehouse Basics I: Equipment & Safety

The words you'll see and hear on your very first shift: pallets, forklifts, gloves, helmets, and the safety signs that keep everyone in one piece.

🎯 By the end of this lesson you can:

  • Name key warehouse equipment and safety gear
  • Recognize warning signs and safety instructions
  • Understand Czech noun gender using ten / ta / to

💡 Tip

These words are among the very first you'll see printed on signs, bins, and forms once you clock in — learning them cold makes your first shift far less stressful.

Step 2 · Grammar focus

Noun gender: ten / ta / to

Every Czech noun has a gender — masculine (ten), feminine (ta), or neuter (to). You can't always guess it, but recognizing the pattern helps you use the right words later.

GenderArticleExamples
Masculinetenten sklad, ten regál, ten skener
Femininetata paleta, ta krabice, ta helma
Neutertoto nářadí, to balení (packaging)

📌 Good to know

For now, just notice the article when you learn a new word — most feminine nouns end in -a, most neuter nouns end in -o or -í, but there are exceptions, so it's worth memorizing each word with its gender from day one.
