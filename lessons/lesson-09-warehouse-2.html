Warehouse Basics II: Work Commands

The short, sharp commands your shift lead or coworkers will use all day long — "take this," "check that," "scan it," "sign here."

🎯 By the end of this lesson you can:

  • Understand and give simple work commands
  • Recognize the imperative (command) form of common verbs
  • Follow a short sequence of workplace instructions
Step 1 · Vocabulary

New words

Each verb is shown as infinitive → command form. Tap the speaker to hear the command as you'd hear it on the floor.

  • vzít → Vezmi!/VE-zmi/to take → Take!
  • dát → Dej!/day/to put/give → Put! / Give!
  • otevřít → Otevři!/O-te-vrzhi/to open → Open!
  • zavřít → Zavři!/ZA-vrzhi/to close → Close!
  • zvednout → Zvedni!/ZVED-nyi/to lift → Lift!
  • položit → Polož!/PO-lozh/to put down → Put down!
  • přinést → Přines!/PRZHI-nes/to bring → Bring!
  • odnést → Odnes!/OD-nes/to carry away → Carry away!
  • zkontrolovat → Zkontroluj!/ZKON-tro-luy/to check → Check!
  • naskenovat → Naskenuj!/NA-ske-nuy/to scan → Scan!
  • podepsat → Podepiš!/PO-de-pish/to sign → Sign!
  • počkat → Počkej!/POCH-kay/to wait → Wait!

💡 Tip

You'll hear these words shouted across the floor far more often than the polite, full-sentence Czech from a textbook. Learn the command form first — it's what actually gets used.

Step 2 · Grammar focus

The imperative (command) form

At work you'll mostly hear short informal commands directed at ty (you). Many are formed by taking the verb and dropping the infinitive "-t" ending, then adjusting the stem — but it's easiest at A1 to simply learn each pair as vocabulary (infinitive → command), as shown above.

InfinitiveCommand (ty)Meaning
vzítVezmi!Take!
otevřítOtevři!Open!
zkontrolovatZkontroluj!Check!
počkatPočkej!Wait!

📌 Good to know

These commands are informal ('ty' form) — completely normal between coworkers on the floor. With a manager you don't know well, you'll more often hear a polite request instead, like "Můžete otevřít krabici?" (Could you open the box?)
