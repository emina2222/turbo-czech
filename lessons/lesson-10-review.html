Step 1 · Grammar recap

Your three core verbs, and noun gender

Three verbs carry most of your A1 Czech: být (to be), mít (to have), and chtít (to want). Here they are side by side, plus the noun-gender pattern that shapes everything else.

být (to be)

PronounForm
já (I)jsem
ty (you, informal)jsi
on/ona (he/she)je
my (we)jsme
vy (you, formal/plural)jste
oni (they)jsou

mít (to have)

PronounForm
já (I)mám
ty (you, informal)máš
on/ona (he/she)má
my (we)máme
vy (you, formal/plural)máte
oni (they)mají

chtít (to want)

PronounForm
já (I)chci
ty (you, informal)chceš
on/ona (he/she)chce
my (we)chceme
vy (you, formal/plural)chcete
oni (they)chtějí

Noun gender

GenderArticleExample
Masculinetenten sklad — the warehouse
Femininetata paleta — the pallet
Neutertoto nářadí — the tool(s)

💡 Two patterns worth remembering

Commands (Lesson 9): drop the pronoun and change the verb ending to give an instruction — Vezmi! (Take!), Otevři! (Open!), Zkontroluj! (Check!).

Counting nouns (Lesson 2): the noun ending changes with the number — 1 krabice (one box), 2–4 krabice (two to four boxes), 5+ krabic (five or more boxes).

Step 2 · Vocabulary refresher

Key vocabulary refresher

Twelve words pulled from across the course — one or two per lesson. Tap the speaker and say each one out loud.

  • Dobrý den/DOB-ree den/hello (formal)
  • Jmenuji se…/ye-MEN-oo-yi seh/my name is…
  • pět/pyet/five
  • rodina/RO-dyi-na/family
  • Chtěl bych / Chtěla bych/khtyel bikh / KHTYE-la bikh/I would like (male / female speaker)
  • sklad/sklat/warehouse
  • Pozor!/PO-zor/watch out! / caution!
  • směna/SMNYEH-na/shift (work)
  • Kde je…?/gdeh yeh/where is…?
  • Kolik to stojí?/KO-lik to STO-yee/how much does it cost?
  • Vezmi!/VEZ-mi/take! (command)
  • Na shledanou/na SKHLE-da-noh/goodbye (formal)
