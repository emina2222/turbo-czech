Noun Gender: Ten / Ta / To

← Back to Grammar

Every Czech noun belongs to one of three groups — masculine, feminine, or neuter. The little words ten, ta, to ("the/this") show you which group a noun is in, and that group affects other words around it (like endings and numbers), so it's worth learning early.

Pattern

Ten (masculine) · Ta (feminine) · To (neuter)

GenderMarkerExamples
Masculinetenten sklad (the warehouse), ten regál (the shelf/rack)
Femininetata paleta (the pallet), ta krabice (the box)
Neutertoto nářadí (the tool), to auto (the car)

Examples

  • Ten sklad je velký.The warehouse is big.
  • Ta paleta je těžká.The pallet is heavy.
  • To auto je nové.The car is new.
  • Ten regál je prázdný.The shelf/rack is empty.

📌 Good to know

A useful first guess: most feminine nouns end in -a (paleta, krabice being an exception you just learn), and most neuter nouns end in -o or -í (auto, nářadí). It's only a guess though — always learn the gender together with each new word, since there are exceptions.
