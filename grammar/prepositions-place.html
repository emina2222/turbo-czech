Prepositions of Place: V & Na

← Back to Grammar

V (in) and na (at/on) are the two most common words for saying where something is. You'll use them dozens of times a day.

Pattern

V (in) vs. Na (at / on)

CzechEnglish
v kancelářiin the office
v šatněin the locker room
na parkovištiin the parking lot
na paletěon the pallet

Examples

  • Jsem v kanceláři.I'm in the office.
  • Kabát je v šatně.The coat is in the locker room.
  • Auto je na parkovišti.The car is in the parking lot.
  • Krabice je na paletě.The box is on the pallet.

These pair naturally with the question word kde (where):

  • Kde je toaleta? — Je tam, vlevo.Where is the toilet? — It's there, on the left.

📌 Good to know

The choice between "v" and "na" isn't always predictable from English — for example, Czech uses "na" for parking lots even though English says "in the parking lot." At A1, it's best learned phrase-by-phrase rather than by a strict rule.
