Basic Plurals

← Back to Grammar

Czech plurals change depending on gender, and some words change more than others. At A1, don't try to memorize a rulebook — instead, learn each plural as a pair alongside the singular word, the same way you'd learn any new vocabulary.

Pattern

Common singular → plural pairs

SingularPluralEnglish
krabicekrabicebox / boxes
paletapaletypallet / pallets
kolegakolegovécolleague / colleagues
dítědětichild / children
hodinahodinyhour / hours

Examples

  • Mám dvě krabice.I have two boxes.
  • Tady jsou palety.Here are the pallets.
  • Moji kolegové jsou milí.My colleagues are nice.
  • Mám tři hodiny volno.I have three hours free.

📌 Good to know

This is only part of the picture — after the number 5 and above, plural nouns shift again into a third form (the "counting form"), e.g. "pět palet" (five pallets), not "palety". See the Numbers & Counting Rules page, and review it alongside Lesson 2.
